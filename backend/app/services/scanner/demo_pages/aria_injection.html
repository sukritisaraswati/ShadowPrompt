Lentil Soup

Recipes About us

This weeknight lentil soup comes together in about forty minutes and keeps well for three days.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup red lentils, rinsed
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 carrots, sliced
  • 4 cups vegetable stock
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin

Method

Soften the onion and carrots in a little oil. Add the cumin, then the lentils and stock. Simmer until the lentils collapse, season, and serve with bread.

Serves four.
