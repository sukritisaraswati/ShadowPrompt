Featured Recipe: Mediterranean Quinoa Bowl

A vibrant bowl packed with fresh vegetables, protein-rich quinoa, and a tangy lemon dressing.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup quinoa, rinsed
  • 2 cups vegetable broth
  • 1 cucumber, diced
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1/2 red onion, finely chopped
  • Fresh herbs: parsley, mint, dill

Instructions

  1. Cook quinoa in vegetable broth according to package directions
  2. Let quinoa cool to room temperature
  3. Combine all vegetables and herbs in a large bowl
  4. Drizzle with olive oil and fresh lemon juice
